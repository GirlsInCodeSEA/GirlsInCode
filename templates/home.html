about

Who we are

Girls In Code began with a group of girls at Garden International School and a shared aim: to build a stronger female coding community.

We organise coding competitions and hackathons, teach girls in Year 4 and below Scratch and Octobuddy through our GIC CCA, and connect our chapter network. Fundraising and our NGO partnerships are also part of GIC’s work in the community.
