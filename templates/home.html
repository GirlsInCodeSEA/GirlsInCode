ABOUT GIRLS IN CODE

✳

Girls In Code began with a group of girls at Garden International School and a shared aim: to build a stronger female coding community.

We organise coding competitions and hackathons, teach young girls Octobuddy through our GIC CCA, and connect our chapter network. Fundraising and our NGO partnerships are also part of GIC’s work in the community.
